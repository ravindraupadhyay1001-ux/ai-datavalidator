Questions

Answers before you ask

Browse the common ones — or ask our AI assistant anything about the product.

What makes AI DataValidator different?
It's built by someone who lived the problem — years in data ops, BA, data quality and reconciliation. It's a tool, not a platform: self-serve, no code, no approvals. The AI drafts the rules while a deterministic engine computes every number exactly — and it gets smarter the more you use it.
Is my data secure?
Yes. It runs in your own environment with per-user isolation, your data is never used to train any model, and every run is logged so you keep a full audit trail.
What sources and formats does it support?
CSV and Excel files, plus databases, data warehouses, cloud storage and REST APIs — through saved connectors. Point it at a source or drop in a file; there's no pipeline to build.
Do I need to write code or SQL?
No. Keys and validation rules are inferred automatically. Adjust anything in the interface, or ask the AI copilot to draft the rules for you.
Can my whole team use it, and who sees the data?
Yes. Everyone gets their own isolated workspace, and admins control roles and access from a Users & Roles panel — so people only see what they should.
Can I track quality and breaks over time?
Yes. Every run is recorded, so you can watch data-quality scores and reconciliation break-rates trend against a baseline — not just a one-off result.
Can I export or download the results?
Yes. Download matched rows, breaks and quality reports to share or archive — your results are never locked in.
What happens after the free trial?
Your trial access pauses, but your saved connections and rules stay intact — nothing is deleted. Just reach out to keep going.
Can I import and reuse my validation rules?
Yes. Save rules as reusable rulesets with tolerances, version them, and apply the same checks across datasets every run.
